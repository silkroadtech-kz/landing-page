Terms

  1. 01

    Reward

    10% of the signed contract value. One payout per client, as many clients as you like.

  2. 02

    When we pay

    As the client pays: once a payment reaches us, we transfer you 10% of it within 5 business days. If the contract is paid in parts, your payouts come in parts too.

  3. 03

    How we pay

    Kaspi, Halyk, or an account at any bank in Kazakhstan.

  4. 04

    Registration

    The client is registered to whoever shared their contact first, for 90 days. If the contract is signed within that period, the reward is yours.

  5. 05

    What doesn't count

    Our current clients and companies we are already talking to. If that happens, we'll tell you right away.

  6. 06

    With the client

    Let the client know we will get in touch. Don't promise prices or timelines — we give those after we've looked at the task.

Do I have to sell?

No. An introduction is enough. We run the meetings, the estimate, the contract, and the development.

How will I know the client is registered to me?

We reply in the same chat within one business day, then keep you posted on the talks and on when the client signs.

What if the client doesn't sign right away?

The client stays registered to you for 90 days from when you shared the contact. If they sign within that period, the 10% is yours.

The contract is paid in parts. When do I get paid?

From every payment. For example, a client pays a 50% advance on a 3,000,000 ₸ contract — you get 150,000 ₸, and the other 150,000 ₸ after the second payment.

How many clients can I refer?

As many as you like. 10% for every signed contract.
