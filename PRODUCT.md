# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Business owners, operations leaders, and technical decision-makers evaluating a digital partner. They need to quickly understand whether Silk Road Tech can own a complex transformation initiative and begin a focused discovery conversation.

## Product Purpose

Silk Road Tech presents its expertise in custom software, AI assistants, data analytics, and hardware-software integration. The site builds confidence in the team's depth and converts qualified B2B visitors into project enquiries and workshops.

## Brand Personality

Confident, precise, human. The voice is direct and technically capable without becoming distant or bureaucratic. Visitors should leave understanding the offer, trusting the team's depth, and wanting to discuss a scoped project.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS templates, blue-purple gradient conventions, inflated product claims, stock-heavy layouts, loud discount-studio sales language, and cold enterprise-vendor bureaucracy.

## Design Principles

1. Make technical depth understandable at a glance.
2. Earn trust with concrete process, people, and proof rather than superlatives.
3. Give every qualified visitor a clear path to start a focused conversation.
4. Balance engineering rigor with direct, human communication.
5. Build a distinct local and international B2B presence without resorting to category cliches.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard-access standards. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`; do not make essential content dependent on motion, color alone, or time-based interactions. Maintain clear Russian and English content paths.
